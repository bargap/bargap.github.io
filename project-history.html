Project History

As a Research Director, I have delivered 178 custom survey research projects to 86 clients since 2023, each one from the first client call through the final presentation. The projects vary widely in industry, subject matter, audience, and method. Most of them required me to learn a new sector quickly enough to scope, design, and deliver a solid study. That is also why I started building my own tooling to work more efficiently. To get a sense of how varied the work is, click Random project a few times.
